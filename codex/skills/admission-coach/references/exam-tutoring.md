# Exam tutoring and extensible exam guides

## Identify the assessment

Establish the exact exam, version/track, subject, language, target year, delivery format, and relevant region. Ask only for fields material to the present task. SAT, IELTS, CSCA, and Gaokao are independent routes, not interchangeable labels. Never assume a student applying to China must take both CSCA and Gaokao, or that either applies without checking the university and applicant route.

For a new exam, use the same process below rather than claiming a preloaded syllabus. Add other exams, language tests, qualifications, and institution-specific interviews when relevant to the student's actual route.

## Establish an exam guide

When building a preparation plan, record these fields in the agreed workspace if persistence is requested:

- Exact assessment/track, subject combination, year/region, and language.
- Applicability to this applicant and target program; mark unresolved requirements.
- Official specification and current assessment objectives, with source URLs and checked dates.
- Verified task types, timing, permitted aids, scoring/rubric, and format.
- Official samples and legitimate practice resources; identify access restrictions.
- Current baseline with date, evidence, testing conditions, and whether official, practice, or tutor-estimated.
- Target, available time, diagnostic gaps, and next review.

Do not fill missing fields from another exam. A source directory is a starting point, not proof of a current rule. Recheck time-sensitive fields for the student's cycle.

| SAT | https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/ and https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/practice/practice-tests | Distinguish official adaptive practice results from locally generated practice accuracy; do not infer a scaled score from an arbitrary question set. |

| CSCA | https://csca.cn/ plus the target university's international undergraduate admissions notice | Verify required subjects, test language, intake, and applicant category. The site may require JavaScript; an unreadable page is not evidence of its rules. |

| Other assessment | Its awarding body/exam authority and the target institution | Establish the guide before claiming exam-specific alignment. |

## Practice loop

1. Use a short representative diagnostic or the student's recent work. For unfamiliar exams, verify task alignment first; otherwise call it general subject practice.
2. Ask for an attempt and reasoning when helpful. Keep solutions hidden until the student attempts, asks for help, or requests a worked example.
3. Identify the main error mechanism and teach the missing step with an example.
4. Ask a related but changed question to test transfer; schedule later retrieval through the agreed plan.
5. Record item provenance, attempt, error type, correction, and remaining uncertainty when records are enabled. Avoid storing whole copyrighted test banks.

Solve generated questions independently before presenting them; check answer uniqueness, units, diagrams, and distractors. Ambiguous or defective questions should be corrected without blaming the learner.

## Score and modality limits

Use an official answer key/conversion only for the matching test form and conditions. Otherwise report raw performance or criterion feedback. Never convert a few practice answers into an official SAT/CSCA/Gaokao score.

For IELTS writing, ground estimated criterion feedback in the submitted response and current descriptors; missing task material limits assessment. For speaking, text can support language/content feedback but cannot establish pronunciation, real-time delivery, or listening ability. Only assess audio dimensions actually accessible to the tools, and label automated feedback as estimated. An unsupervised exercise cannot establish an official score.
